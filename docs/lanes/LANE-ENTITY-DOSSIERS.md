# Entity Dossiers: Dutch Party-Finance Register Donors

**Date of research:** 20 November 2026
**Method:** Read-only web research via search engines, public court records (uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl), company-registry aggregators, news archives, and entity websites. No paid databases were queried. Every fact carries a URL. What could not be verified from public sources is stated as "nothing found".

**Scope rules applied:** No private individuals profiled beyond published corporate roles; no speculation about influence, motive or agendas; no connection of an entity to a political position unless the entity itself published it or the entity appears by name in public party-finance records.

## 1. Chipshol 2000 B.V.

- **Registration:** Part of the Chipshol group; the group holding identified in public registries is Chipshol Holding B.V., KvK 34095034, Schiphol-Rijk, SBI financial holdings ([Company.info](https://companyinfo.nl/organisatieprofiel/activiteiten-van-financiele-holdings/chipshol-holding-b-v-schiphol-rijk-34095034-000004459237)). A separate KvK number for the "Chipshol 2000 B.V." legal entity itself was not found in free public sources.
- **Activity:** Dutch area development company founded in 1986 by Jan Poot in The Hague; owns land around Schiphol Airport for residential and commercial development ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chipshol)). Planned projects: Airport City Badhoevedorp South, Chipshol Park, High Speed Logistics Park, Hub 3.0 Data Center Campus ([Chipshol — Management](https://www.chipshol.com/company-profile-and-policy/management/)).
- **Leadership (published):** Peter Poot, CEO since 1995 (joined 1988, earlier secretary of the board and CFO), son of founder Jan Poot ([Chipshol — Management](https://www.chipshol.com/company-profile-and-policy/management/)).
- **Public litigation:** Well-documented, decades-long land dispute with the state, Schiphol and others ("Chipshol-affaire"), starting 1993: roughly one hundred lawsuits over land expropriation and rezoning around Schiphol; culminated in prosecution of two (former) court officials for perjury — the first such prosecution in Dutch legal history — ending in acquittals. Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chipshol), [NRC 2020](https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/2020/01/08/chipshol-krijgt-19-miljoen-euro-schadevergoeding-van-lvnl-a3986098), [NU.nl](https://www.nu.nl/algemeen/3721300/schadevergoeding-voor-vrijgesproken-oud-rechters-chipsholzaak.html), [Volkskrant](https://www.volkskrant.nl/nieuws-achtergrond/schadeclaim-van-406-miljoen-tegen-gemeente-haarlemmermeer~b6d507ca/). Court judgments name Chipshol entities in multiple proceedings, e.g. ECLI:NL:RBHAA:2004:AO2105, ECLI:NL:GHAMS:2007:BB8851, ECLI:NL:HR:2018:1972 ([uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl](https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/details?id=ECLI%3ANL%3AHR%3A2018%3A1972)). In January 2020 the court awarded Chipshol entities ~€19 million damages from air-traffic-control body LVNL ([NRC](https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/2020/01/08/chipshol-krijgt-19-miljoen-euro-schadevergoeding-van-lvnl-a3986098), [Rechtspraak nieuws](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/rechtbanken/rechtbank-noord-holland/nieuws/2020/01/ruim-19-miljoen-schadevergoeding-voor-chipshol)).
- **Advocacy positions published by the entity:** none found.

## Method note & limitations

- All facts above were traced to public web sources: Dutch court records, entity websites, registry-aggregator previews, and news outlets. No paid registry extracts were purchased; several KvK numbers could therefore not be confirmed, and director/governance data behind paywalls is marked "nothing found" rather than guessed.
- Where multiple same-named entities exist (Mega Management, Movet Beheer), the dossier records the caveat explicitly rather than assuming the donor entity's identity.
- Nothing was found linking any of these entities to published political/advocacy positions beyond the party-finance appearance that earned the entity a place on this list; per the task rules, no further linkage is drawn.
