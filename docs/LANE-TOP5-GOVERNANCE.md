# Governance dossiers — five Dutch state-funded institutes

**Method.** Read-only desk research (20 Nov 2026). Primary sources are each institute's own published annual report / financial statements (PDF URLs given per dossier) plus one primary public register (KvK/Business Register, or the government portal organisaties.overheid.nl where the institute itself cites it). Nothing is inferred; where the institute publishes nothing on a requested item, that is stated as **not published**. Quoted figures are exactly as categorised in the source document (currency €, source stating "x €1.000" where noted). No screenshot facilities were used; every claim is traceable to the cited URL.

---
