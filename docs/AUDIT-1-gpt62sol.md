The SP and VVD evidence checks do not establish individual transactions or end-to-end payment flows. The source for the asserted 2021–2024 PVV subsidy zero was spot-checked against the 2024 report.

I did not inspect a browser screenshot or the deployed site. I traced generator entry points and prerequisites without executing write-producing generators or verifying byte-identical rebuilds.
